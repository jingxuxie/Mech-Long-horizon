## Main discovery

Initial information sufficiency and persistent execution cost are distinct. A state projection can preserve the only initially varying bit while deleting relays needed later. In the nonnegative class, the exact minimum is the union of protocol-compatible source-to-output walks fitting inside the horizon, not merely the initially distinguishable coordinates.

Signed cancellations provide a second distinction: an exact mask at one parameter setting may disappear under coefficient perturbations. The finite-radius theorem handles repeated use of the same recurrent edge without incorrectly treating different time steps as independent parameters.
